--- title: "WBF Commentary: Law 32 – Double and Redouble Out of Rotation" short: "Double and Redouble Out of Rotation" num: "32" weight: 343 ---

If a (re)double out of rotation is inadmissible the TD deals with it using Law 36B. Otherwise, the approach is similar to the one in Law 31: If the offender succeeds in making a comparable call the auction continues normally, if not partner has to pass once.

Example 40:

W      N     E     S
1S     Pass  -     X

South is in the fortunate position that whatever call East makes, a double will still qualify as a comparable call, expressing a similar or more precise meaning (providing the double of a higherlevel bid would still systemically take-out).
