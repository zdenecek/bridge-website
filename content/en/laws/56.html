--- title: "Law 56: Defender’s lead out of turn" short: "Defender’s lead out of turn" heading: "DEFENDER’S LEAD OUT OF TURN" law: 56 weight: 56 translationKey: "law-56" rev2024: false ---

When a lead out of turn is faced, declarer may:

  1. Accept the irregular lead as provided in Law 53, or
  2. Require the defender to retract his faced lead out of turn. The withdrawn card becomes a major penalty card and Law 50D applies.
