The 2017 Laws of Duplicate Bridge of the World Bridge Federation, including the 2024 amendments to Laws 73 and 89. Each law is on its own page; the list on the left stays visible, and the search box searches the text of all laws (type a reference such as 64A2 to jump straight to it).

The English text follows the HTML edition of the Polish Bridge Union (PZBS).

Below each Law are the matching sections of the EBU White Book and the WBF Commentary. Both are also available as separate sections, and the search can include them when you tick the boxes under the search field.
