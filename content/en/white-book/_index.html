The White Book of the English Bridge Union: the EBU's guidance for players, tournament directors and appeals committees, and its interpretations of the Laws. This is the edition effective from 1 August 2025. It is official only for events run by the EBU, but its chapter 8 explains many Laws in ways that are useful everywhere. Each Law page also shows the relevant chapter 8 sections below the Law.
